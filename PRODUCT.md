# Alwankhat — Product & Brand Context

## Brand
- **Name:** Alwankhat (الوان خط)
- **Meaning:** "Colors of the line" — *alwan* (colors) + *khat* (calligraphy/line)
- **Type:** Handmade paintings & calligraphy — original, one-of-a-kind artwork
- **Location:** India
- **Instagram:** @alwankhat

## What we sell
- Original paintings and calligraphy pieces, each unique (stock of 1)
- Categories: **Paintings**, **Calligraphy**
- Sold-out pieces stay visible with a "Sold" badge (gallery feel), never deleted
- Custom commissions as a future option

## Audience
- Art lovers, interior decorators, gifting occasions, Islamic-art collectors
- India-first buyers, one-click Razorpay checkout

## Voice & personality
- Elegant, feminine, warm, artistic
- Premium but heartfelt — the art is the hero
- Calm, refined copy; no hype, no corporate-speak

## What we are NOT
- Not a merch shop (no t-shirts/mugs)
- Not a generic SaaS/startup template
- Not corporate or minimalist-cold
